# AgriConnect demonstration checklist

## Accounts

Create dedicated demo accounts through the registration flow. Assign agent, cooperative-manager and admin roles only through a trusted administrative process; never accept those roles from public registration.

## Golden path

1. Register a farmer and record account consent.
2. Publish a produce listing in Vhembe.
3. Sign in as a buyer and find the listing in the marketplace.
4. Request a quantity no greater than the displayed available stock.
5. Sign in as the farmer and accept the request.
6. Confirm that the order exists and the listing's reserved quantity increased.
7. Use a buyer account to combine active listings from at least two different farmers.
8. Verify the allocations and stock reservations for both farmers.
9. Test offline listing capture and duplicate-safe sync after the offline module is enabled.
10. Sign in as an admin and verify moderation and actual dashboard counts.

## Demo data

Use only synthetic accounts and clearly labelled sample produce. Do not invent farmer testimonials, buyers, revenue or impact.
