# AgriConnect pilot limitations

- Initial geography is Vhembe District, Limpopo.
- Listing quantities, prices and quality notes are farmer-provided and not independently verified.
- The MVP does not collect, escrow or transfer payments and does not claim payment occurred.
- No buyer, price, income or market outcome is guaranteed.
- Exact farm coordinates and private phone numbers are not shown on marketplace listings.
- Offline mode is create-only; edits require connectivity. Drafts remain on-device until a successful idempotent sync is implemented.
- The second locale is scaffolded but must not be represented as reviewed until fluent local speakers approve it.
- SMS, USSD, voice, route optimisation, lending, crop diagnosis and public indigenous-knowledge publishing are outside the first MVP.
- Demo records must be synthetic and clearly labelled. Never present sample data as real farmer participation or impact.
- Obtain POPIA and community-governance review before a commercial pilot.
